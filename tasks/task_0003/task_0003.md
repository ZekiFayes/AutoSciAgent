# Task 03: 3D Steady-State Anisotropic Heat Conduction in a Wood Sample

## Governing 3D Spatially Varying PDE & Boundary Conditions
Without internal heat generation, 3D steady-state heat conduction within a wood sample over $L_x \times L_y \times L_z$ ($L_x = L_y \gg L_z$) is governed by:
$$\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left( k_{x} \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} \right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left( k_{y} \frac{\partial T}{\partial y} \right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \left( k_{z} \frac{\partial T}{\partial z} \right) = 0$$
- **Bottom Surface ($z = 0$, Dirichlet BC)**: $T(x, y, z=0) = T_{\text{bed}}(x,y)$
- **Top Surface ($z = L_z$, Robin Convection BC)**: $-k_{\text{top}} \frac{\partial T}{\partial z}\big|_{z=L_z} = h(T_{\text{top}} - T_{\infty})$
- **Lateral Surfaces ($x = 0, L_x$ and $y = 0, L_y$, Adiabatic Neumann BCs)**: $\frac{\partial T}{\partial x} = 0, \frac{\partial T}{\partial y} = 0$
