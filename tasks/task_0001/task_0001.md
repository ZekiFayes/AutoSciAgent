# Task 01: 1D Transient Heat Conduction Equation

## Governing 1D Parabolic PDE
One-dimensional transient heat conduction along a rod of length $L_x$ over $t \in [0, t_{\text{end}}]$ is governed by:
$$\rho c_p \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left( k_x \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} \right) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \alpha \frac{\partial^2 T}{\partial x^2}, \qquad \alpha = \frac{k_x}{\rho c_p}$$
with Dirichlet boundary conditions $T(0, t) = T_{\text{left}}$, $T(L_x, t) = T_{\text{right}}$, and initial condition $T(x, 0) = T_{\text{init}}$.
